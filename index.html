Healthy Diet Tips

🥗 Eat More Fiber

Include vegetables, beans, and whole grains in every meal.

🚫 Limit Sugar

Avoid sweets, sodas, and packaged juices that spike glucose.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly — at least 8 glasses a day.

🏃 Stay Active

30 minutes of walking daily improves insulin sensitivity.

🍽 Small Portions

Eat smaller meals more often to keep sugar levels stable.

😴 Sleep Well

Aim for 7–8 hours of quality sleep every night.
